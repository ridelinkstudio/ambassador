Questions and answers

Before you apply

Is there a joining or registration fee?

No. The programme flyer states that there are no joining fees or membership charges.

Do I have to design or deliver the services?

No. Your role is to promote the services and refer interested customers. The RideLink/XTC Studio team handles the service work and delivery.

How much commission can I earn?

The advertised commission is 40% on eligible sales. Earnings depend on completed, verified eligible sales and the programme's commission terms.

Where can I promote the services?

You can promote through channels such as WhatsApp, Facebook and your personal or professional networks.

Will I receive training and marketing materials?

Yes. Free training, promotional materials, useful resources and ongoing guidance are included in the programme offer.

How do I join?

Complete the ambassador application form using the Apply button on this page. The team can then follow up with the next steps.
